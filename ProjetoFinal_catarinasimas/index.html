About HabitDesk

About HabitDesk

HabitDesk is a web application designed to help you track your habits, manage your schedule, and improve your overall wellness. Inspired by the classic Windows 95 interface, HabitDesk combines nostalgia with modern functionality to provide a unique user experience.

Features:

  • Habit Tracking: Create and monitor your daily, weekly, or monthly habits.
  • Expense Management: Keep track of your expenses with an easy-to-use interface.
  • Calendar & Scheduling: Organize your events and appointments efficiently.
  • Wellness Logging: Record your mood and sleep patterns to maintain a healthy lifestyle.
  • Customizable Desktop: Personalize your desktop with various settings and themes.
